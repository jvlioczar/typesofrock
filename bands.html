The greatest rock bands of all time

Rock is not a straight line, and no ranking will settle the matter. The criteria here cross cultural impact, repertoire, innovation, influence, longevity and popularity. The idea is to open a conversation.

S

The Titans of rock

Absolute and undisputed. They helped define rock and its branches, and nearly the whole tree carries some of their genes.

A

The Gods of rock

Pillars of rock history whose legacy and influence still echo, even without the Titans' foundational impact.

B

The legends of rock

Hugely popular or fundamental in their subgenres. Many defined an entire branch of the tree.

C

Other great names in rock

Bands of wide reach or real relevance in their niches, completing the global picture of the genre.
