About

The project

Types of Rock is a map of the genre and all its branches, written with the theory of evolution as a lens. It is a BiblioVerse special project.

The genealogy

Parents and children indicate direct influence and are editorial choices based on each genre's most accepted history. The solid line marks the main ancestor and the dashed ones show the others. Dates point to the first recognizable form of the genre and are approximate.

The sonic genome

The eight traits run from 0 to 10 and are editorial estimates, not acoustic measurements. They are for comparing genres, not for decreeing anything.

The lists

The order follows each artist's popularity and relevance in the genre, and the highlighted names form the core of the list. An artist can appear in several genres, and some border cases are included for their link to the scene. Links open a YouTube search.

The sound sketches

The listen buttons generate a synthesized loop in your browser. There are no recordings on the site, and the result is a teaching caricature of tempo, rhythm and distortion.

Privacy policy

In short: Types of Rock collects nothing about you.

What we don't do

The site has no sign-ups, forms, first-party cookies, analytics or ad tracking. We neither request nor keep personal data.

What stays on your device

The English version stores nothing in your browser. The sound sketches and the Lab's math run in your own browser and send nothing.

Services involved

To load the pages, your browser connects to GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts (fonts) and biblioverse.org (the BiblioVerse bar at the top). These services receive the usual technical data of any visit, such as your IP address, and follow their own privacy policies.

External links

Artist and song links open a YouTube search, which follows YouTube's own rules. The site doesn't know whether you clicked.

Your rights

Since we keep no personal data, there is no record of you here to access, correct or delete. You can exercise your data rights directly with the services named above.
