The environment

Context

In the squats and anarchist communities of Thatcher's England, Amebix and Antisect brought metal's weight into political punk. The dirty look and off-grid life remain part of the scene.

The phenotype

Sound

Low, dirty guitars, d-beat, guttural vocals and lyrics about war, ecology and animal rights.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Among genres with little interest in selling, crust is the most radical. It differs from hardcore through weight, and from metal through total rejection of technique and spectacle.
