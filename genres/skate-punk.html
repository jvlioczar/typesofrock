Skate Punk

Emerged 1981Habitat Southern CaliforniaClade Rupture
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Hardcore Punk → Skate Punk
The environment

Context

In California, skaters joined hardcore to empty-pool culture. Brett Gurewitz's Epitaph Records and the Tony Hawk's Pro Skater games took the sound around the world.

The phenotype

Sound

Fast d-beat-style drums, melodic power chords, harmonized vocals and ironic or political lyrics.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is a niche created by a sport. It has hardcore's speed but keeps more melody and technique and less rage, the path to pop-punk.
