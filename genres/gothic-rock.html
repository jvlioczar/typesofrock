The environment

Context

Bauhaus's "Bela Lugosi's Dead" (1979) is seen as the starting point. In the grey Thatcher-era UK, goth subculture offered decadent romanticism, horror literature and an unmistakable look, centered on London's Batcave club.

The phenotype

Sound

Pulsing bass, ethereal guitars, ritualistic drum machines and a low, theatrical voice. It is music about atmosphere and shadow.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It inherits post-punk's prominent bass and glam's theatricality, and replaces rage with melancholy. It differs from metal through its lack of weight: the darkness here comes from mood, not guitar.
