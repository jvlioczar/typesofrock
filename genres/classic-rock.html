The environment

Context

The label was born on 1980s American FM radio to gather the album repertoire of 1965 to 1980. More than a style, it is the era in which the LP became an art form, festivals became mass rites and the rock band became an institution.

The phenotype

Sound

Medium-drive guitars, memorable riffs, solid bass and drums, supporting keyboards, strong vocals and choruses built for stadiums.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is defined less by technique than by natural selection: it is what survived on the radio. It is broader than hard rock and less experimental than progressive, and works as the thick trunk everything seems to spring from.
