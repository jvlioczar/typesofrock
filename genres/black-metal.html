Black Metal

The environment

Context

The first wave came with Venom and Bathory. The second rose in early-1990s Norway, in geographic isolation, from a small scene rejecting society and Christianity, marked by church burnings and real crimes that still stain its history.

The phenotype

Sound

Tremolo picking, blast beats, shrieking high vocals and deliberately raw production that turns the guitar into fog.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It differs from death metal in using high pitch instead of low, and atmosphere instead of technique. It is a textbook example of speciation by isolation: cold, distance and hostility selected for a unique sound.
