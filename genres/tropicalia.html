The environment

Context

Under Brazil's military dictatorship, Caetano Veloso, Gilberto Gil and Os Mutantes drew on Oswald de Andrade's cultural cannibalism: devour the foreign guitar without abandoning Brazil. The movement was short, as Caetano and Gil were jailed and exiled, but it transformed Brazilian music.

The phenotype

Sound

Fuzz guitar next to berimbau and Rogério Duprat's orchestral arrangements, a collage of bolero, baião and psychedelia, always with irony and popular melody.

The mutation

What sets it apart

In a setting where part of the cultural left rejected the electric guitar as imperialist, this hybridization was a political act. It differs from British or American psychedelia through its carnival collage.
