Funk Rock

Emerged 1968Habitat USAClade Trunk

The environment

Context

In the years of the civil rights struggle and psychedelic experiment, Black artists like Sly Stone and George Clinton connected rock guitar to James Brown's groove. In the 1980s the fusion reignited in Los Angeles and gave rise to funk metal.

The phenotype

Sound

Slap bass up front, syncopated drums with emphasis on the one, distorted guitar alternating between riff and wah-wah funk.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Where rock in general marks the backbeat, here the engine is bass syncopation. It differs from pure funk through the weight and distortion of the guitar.
