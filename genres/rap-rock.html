The environment

Context

In 1986 the meeting of Run-DMC and Aerosmith on "Walk This Way" broke a radio barrier, and Beastie Boys sold millions. In the 1990s Rage Against the Machine joined the genre to activism, and a generation came to hear hip-hop and rock as equals.

The phenotype

Sound

Distorted riffs with rhymed vocals, hip-hop drum patterns, DJ and samples integrated into the band.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Horizontal gene transfer in its pure state: an entire culture, hip-hop, passes into rock at once. Without the melodic singer the voice turns rhythmic, changing the song's whole architecture.
