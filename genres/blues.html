Blues

Emerged 1900Habitat Mississippi Delta, USAClade Roots
Root of the tree: older than rock itself.
The environment

Context

It emerged among descendants of enslaved Africans in the American South after abolition, out of work songs and spirituals. The Great Migration carried it to Chicago, where it swapped acoustic for electric guitar and prepared the ground for everything that followed.

The phenotype

Sound

Twelve bars, call and response between voice and instrument, and blue notes: pitches that fall between piano keys and sound like a moan. The form is deliberately simple, because what matters is tone, delay and inflection.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is the most important common ancestor on this tree. Nearly every rock riff carries its pentatonic scale, and blues stands apart from its descendants by putting personal expression ahead of volume and speed.
