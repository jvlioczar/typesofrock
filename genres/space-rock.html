Space Rock

Emerged 1967Habitat LondonClade Expansion
Folk → Folk Rock → Psychedelic Rock → Space Rock
The environment

Context

The space race fed the imagination of the time. Pink Floyd led the way in 1967 with "Interstellar Overdrive", and Hawkwind turned the idea into a spectacle of science fiction and lights.

The phenotype

Sound

Synths imitating spacecraft, long echoes, repeated riffs at a steady tempo and lyrics about the cosmos.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is psychedelia with another destination: instead of an inner trip, a trip through space. Its repetition is closer to krautrock than to blues solos.
