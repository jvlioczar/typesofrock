Proto-Punk

Emerged 1967Habitat Detroit and New YorkClade Rupture
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Proto-Punk
The environment

Context

In declining Detroit and Warhol's New York, bands played too loud for the hippie dream. They almost always failed commercially, but their records became sacred texts for the class of 1976.

The phenotype

Sound

Raw, repetitive riffs, feedback, spat vocals, poor production and lyrics about boredom, drugs and urban violence.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is a transitional link: it already has punk's sound and attitude but not yet the scene or manifesto. Like intermediate fossils, it was only named after the next species appeared.
