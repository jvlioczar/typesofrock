The environment

Context

In Tokyo, X Japan took glam and metal into an aesthetic of its own, with extreme costumes, makeup and androgyny. Needing no Western market, the scene survived as an independent ecosystem.

The phenotype

Sound

A blend of fast metal, orchestral ballads and gothic rock, with dramatic vocals.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Another case of island isolation: in the Japanese archipelago, display was selected so strongly that it came to define the genre itself, far beyond the original glam.
