Gothic Metal

Emerged 1990Habitat EnglandClade Weight
Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Heavy Metal → Doom Metal → Gothic Metal
The environment

Context

In Halifax, Paradise Lost released Gothic in 1991 and showed that metal could flirt with melancholy. In 1990s Europe the tension between female and male voices became a scene signature.

The phenotype

Sound

Slow guitars, church keyboards, a low voice beside an ethereal female voice in "beauty and the beast" duality.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Metal gained romanticism from gothic rock, and gothic rock gained weight. It was a stable hybrid that paved the way for symphonic metal.
