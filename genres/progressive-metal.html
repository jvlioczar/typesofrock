Progressive Metal

Emerged 1985Habitat USAClade Weight

The environment

Context

Queensrÿche and Fates Warning started from melodic metal and sought Rush's legacy, and Dream Theater's Berklee students consolidated the formula. The niche stayed off the radio, with a loyal audience of musicians.

The phenotype

Sound

Odd meters over distorted riffs, synchronized guitar and keyboard solos, long tracks and concept albums.

The mutation

What sets it apart

A case of improbable hybridization: the virtuosity punk tried to kill survives here with metal's low tuning. It differs from classic progressive through weight and rhythmic aggression.
