The environment

Context

After Cobain's death, labels sought bands with that weight but less pain. Foo Fighters, Bush, Creed and later Nickelback dominated rock radio until the end of the following decade.

The phenotype

Sound

Cleaner guitars than grunge, thick vocals, big choruses and polished production.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is domestication through market pressure: traits that made grunge uncomfortable were selected against. The sound stayed and the conflict left.
