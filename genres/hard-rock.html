The environment

Context

When blues rock met more powerful Marshall amps, volume became an end in itself. Cream, Hendrix, Led Zeppelin and Deep Purple filled arenas in the late 1960s as the hippie dream cooled and audiences wanted catharsis.

The phenotype

Sound

Power-chord riffs, saturated guitar, heavy drums, high vocals and solos. Compared with blues rock, the structure closes up and the chorus begs to be shouted.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It inherited the pentatonic from blues rock but put the riff in charge. It differs from heavy metal by keeping the swing and sensuality of blues, without metal's darkness and mechanical precision.
