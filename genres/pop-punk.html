Pop-Punk

Emerged 1978Habitat England and CaliforniaClade Rupture
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Pop-Punk
The environment

Context

Buzzcocks and Ramones already contained melody, and the California scene developed it on labels like Lookout!. In 1994 Green Day and The Offspring sold millions. In the 2000s Blink-182 and Avril Lavigne became global teen culture through MTV.

The phenotype

Sound

Fast power chords, nasal vocals, sticky choruses, skate-punk drumming and lyrics about romance and suburban boredom.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is domestication in the Darwinian sense: punk bred for melody until it became a friendly creature. It differs from punk through lighter themes and clean production, and from emo through euphoria.
