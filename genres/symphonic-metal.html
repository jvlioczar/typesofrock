Symphonic Metal

Emerged 1992Habitat Finland and the NetherlandsClade Weight
Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Heavy Metal → Doom Metal → Gothic Metal → Symphonic Metal
The environment

Context

Therion blended orchestra and death metal in the early 1990s and in 1996 Nightwish arrived with Tarja Turunen's operatic voice. Large audiences in Finland, the Netherlands and Germany sustained growth, with Evanescence and Within Temptation reaching the charts.

The phenotype

Sound

Orchestra and choir, real or synthesized, female operatic vocals, fast-picked guitars and theatrical structures.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is not just metal with keyboards: the orchestral element is central. It differs from power metal through theatrical darkness and from gothic metal through grandeur.
