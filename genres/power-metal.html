The environment

Context

With Keeper of the Seven Keys, Helloween created fast, luminous metal in 1980s Germany. The pressure was cultural: while the rest of metal shut itself in gloom, central Europe and the Nordic countries embraced epic and fantasy. Brazil contributed Angra.

The phenotype

Sound

Fast double bass, clean high vocals, neoclassical keyboards, anthem choruses and themes of heroic fantasy.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It occupies the niche opposite thrash and death: it keeps speed but trades aggression for melody and euphoria. It is more positive, epic and singable.
