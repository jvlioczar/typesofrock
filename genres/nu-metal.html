Nu Metal

Crossover / fusion of Alternative Metal, Rap Rock / Rap Metal, Industrial Metal and Hip-hop

The environment

Context

Korn's 1994 debut, music of adolescent trauma and pain, created a new sound. In the late 1990s MTV, TRL and the Ozzfest festival drove the genre to sell tens of millions. It was panned by adults and loved by youth.

The phenotype

Sound

Very low seven-string guitars, few solos, hip-hop beats, DJ, rhythm rather than riff and vocals between screams, rap and confession.

The mutation

What sets it apart

The guitar solo, which seemed an essential gene of metal, simply vanished. This erasure, along with hip-hop and catharsis, is nu metal's main mutation.
