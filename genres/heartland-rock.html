The environment

Context

In the American industrial crisis of the 1970s and 1980s, Springsteen and others sang about closed factories, small towns and highways. Born to Run and Born in the U.S.A. made this a mass phenomenon.

The phenotype

Sound

Clear guitar, organ, occasional sax, firm drums and a hoarse voice telling working people's stories.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Its trait is narrative: the theme is ordinary life, and geography lies in the lyrics. It differs from arena rock through sobriety and the social concern inherited from folk.
