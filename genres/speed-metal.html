Speed Metal

Emerged 1979Habitat England and GermanyClade Extreme
Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Heavy Metal → New Wave of British Heavy Metal → Speed Metal
The environment

Context

Motörhead joined metal's weight to punk's rush, and songs like Accept's "Fast as a Shark" and Judas Priest's "Exciter" marked a new limit of tempo.

The phenotype

Sound

High-speed double bass, fast picking, neoclassical solos and high vocals.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is the evolutionary stage between NWOBHM and thrash. It keeps the melody of traditional metal, which thrash would abandon for aggression.
