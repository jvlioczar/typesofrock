Metalcore

Crossover / fusion of Hardcore Punk, Thrash Metal and Post-Hardcore
Type Crossover / fusionEmerged 1990Habitat US NortheastClade Extreme
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Hardcore Punk → Metalcore
The environment

Context

Hardcore bands from New York and Boston began incorporating metal. In the 2000s, under the influence of Swedish melodic death metal, Killswitch Engage and Bring Me the Horizon reached mass audiences.

The phenotype

Sound

The breakdown is the hallmark: a rhythmic drop of palm-muted riffs made for the pit. It includes harmonized melodic guitars and alternates screams with clean choruses.

The mutation

What sets it apart

From hardcore it inherits attitude and mosh pit; from metal, technique. The breakdown separates it from thrash, and the clean chorus from deathcore.
