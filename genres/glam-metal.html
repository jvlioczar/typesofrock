Glam Metal

Emerged 1981Habitat Sunset Strip, Los AngelesClade Weight
Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Glam Rock → Glam Metal
The environment

Context

On the 1980s Sunset Strip, MTV and Reagan-era excess selected for hair, makeup and endless parties. It was an enormous commercial reign that ended abruptly with grunge, in a near mass extinction in the 1990s.

The phenotype

Sound

Guitar tapping, fast solos, power ballads, choral hooks, roomy production with loads of reverb.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It mixes metal's weight with glam's aesthetic. It differs from thrash, its natural enemy, by seeking the party rather than confrontation, and from hard rock in display taken to the maximum.
