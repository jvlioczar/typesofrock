The environment

Context

Skyclad inaugurated the genre in 1991, but it took shape in the Nordic countries. It is a local answer to globalization, with bands seeking their own Celtic, Viking or Baltic past.

The phenotype

Sound

Metal riffs with traditional instruments such as flute, fiddle, pipes and accordion, and vocals that are sometimes harsh, sometimes tavern-like.

The mutation

What sets it apart

The same trunk generates a regional species in each country, a local adaptation. It differs from power metal through its traditional roots.
