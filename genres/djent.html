The environment

Context

Sweden's Meshuggah created the language in the 1990s, but the genre was born with the internet. Guitarists like Periphery's Misha Mansoor swapped ideas on forums and recorded at home using simulators. It is the first rock genre born on personal computers.

The phenotype

Sound

The name imitates the sound of the picking: djent. Eight-string guitars, polymeters over a fixed pulse, surgical production.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Technology is the environment here: cheap plugins permit low-end control and precision. The riff becomes a mathematical pattern. It differs from progressive metal through its muted tone and polymeters.
