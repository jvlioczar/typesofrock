Garage Rock

Emerged 1963Habitat American suburbsClade Trunk
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock
The environment

Context

After the Beatles, thousands of American teenagers formed bands in their home garages with cheap gear and no training. The 1960s scene survives on regional singles, and it returned in the early 2000s through the new wave of The Strokes, The White Stripes and Arctic Monkeys.

The phenotype

Sound

Saturated guitars and accidental fuzz, cheap organ, noisy recordings, shouted vocals and two or three chords played with urgency.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Amateurism is the defining feature, not a flaw. It differs from beat through its lack of polish and harmony, and it is the direct link to punk, which would turn this limitation into a manifesto.
