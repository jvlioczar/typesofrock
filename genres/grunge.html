The environment

Context

Rainy Seattle, far from the touring circuit, cultivated in isolation a blend of punk and Black Sabbath, documented by the Sub Pop label. In 1991 Nevermind knocked Michael Jackson off the Billboard top and made glam metal vanish. Kurt Cobain's death in 1994 marked the symbolic end.

The phenotype

Sound

Dirty heavy guitars, quiet/loud dynamics, torn voices and lyrics about apathy and depression. Flannel and denim instead of makeup.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Another case of geographic isolation and also of extinction by competition: within months the audience swapped glam for grunge. It rejects spectacle, and this refusal is its strongest trait.
