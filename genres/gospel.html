Gospel

Emerged 1920Habitat Black churches of the US SouthClade Roots
Root of the tree: older than rock itself.
The environment

Context

It took shape in Black American churches in the early twentieth century, as Thomas Dorsey joined devotion to the swing of blues. It was communal music built to lift a congregation into ecstasy, and it trained many of the singers who later invented soul and rock'n'roll.

The phenotype

Sound

Call-and-response choirs, off-beat clapping, hammered piano and organ, and voices climbing to the edge of a scream. Sister Rosetta Tharpe was already adding electric guitar in the 1930s and 1940s.

The mutation

What sets it apart

The gene gospel passed on is performance as trance: Little Richard's shriek and the physical surrender of the stage come from church. It differs from blues through its collective tone and hope, where blues tends to be intimate and melancholic.
