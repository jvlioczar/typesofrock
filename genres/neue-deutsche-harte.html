Neue Deutsche Härte

Emerged 1994Habitat GermanyClade Alternative

The environment

Context

After reunification, German bands combined metal and electronic music, singing in their own language. Rammstein became the scene's largest export, with real-fire shows.

The phenotype

Sound

Heavy, simple unison riffs, a martial beat, synths, a low voice and a declamatory tone in German.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Language and march are its own traits: rather than hide the accent, the genre turns it into a weapon. It differs from industrial metal through a simplicity built for stadiums.
