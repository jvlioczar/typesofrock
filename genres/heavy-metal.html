Heavy Metal

Emerged 1969Habitat Birmingham, EnglandClade Weight
Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Heavy Metal
The environment

Context

In industrial, working-class Birmingham, Black Sabbath created a rock that sounded like the factories its members had worked in. Tony Iommi lost his fingertips in a press and tuned his guitar down, and the accident became a trait of the whole genre.

The phenotype

Sound

Low tuning, the tritone, power chords, precise picking, double-bass drums, operatic vocals and themes of horror, war and myth.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is a clear mutation of hard rock: it drops the swing of blues, trades sensual for somber and adopts precision. It became the branch with the most descendants after 1980.
