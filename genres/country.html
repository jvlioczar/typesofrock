Country

Emerged 1922Habitat Appalachia and the US SouthClade Roots
Root of the tree: older than rock itself.
The environment

Context

It grew from the rural music of poor white Southerners, a blend of British ballads, the African-derived banjo and blues. Radio and the first records of the 1920s turned a regional tradition into an industry, with Nashville as its capital.

The phenotype

Sound

Acoustic guitars, steel guitar, fiddle, nasal vocals and direct stories about love, work and loss. The loping boom-chick rhythm and the twang of the Telecaster are its most inherited traits.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It gave rock the twang and the craft of telling a story in three minutes. It parts from blues through clearer harmony and regular meter, and the cross between the two produced rockabilly.
