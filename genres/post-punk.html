Post-Punk

Emerged 1977Habitat Manchester, London and New YorkClade Alternative
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Post-Punk
The environment

Context

Punk opened the door and soon felt limited. In British cities hit by Thatcher-era unemployment, ex-punks and art students began reading theory, listening to dub and krautrock and looking for stranger music.

The phenotype

Sound

Melodic bass up front, angular chorused guitars, dry or electronic drums, distant vocals and lyrics about alienation.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It has punk's attitude and DIY but not its three chords. It is one of the most fertile divergences on the tree: gothic, alternative and indie all come from it.
