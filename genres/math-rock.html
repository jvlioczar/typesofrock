Math Rock

Emerged 1988Habitat Chicago and Louisville, USAClade Alternative
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Hardcore Punk → Post-Hardcore → Math Rock
The environment

Context

In the basements of American cities, musicians with hardcore roots wanted the complexity of prog and King Crimson without the fantasy or the costumes. Slint and Don Caballero marked the scene, which later resonated in Japan too.

The phenotype

Sound

Irregular meters, clean tapped guitars, rhythmic patterns that change without warning, many instrumental tracks.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is pocket-sized prog: rhythmic complexity remains but the epic disappears. The precision is uneasy and the mood drier and smaller.
