Oi! / Street Punk

Emerged 1977Habitat East LondonClade Rupture
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Oi! / Street Punk
The environment

Context

As art punk moved into studios, youths in British working-class districts wanted music of their own. Part of the scene was associated with far-right groups, something many bands openly fought.

The phenotype

Sound

Simple guitars, choruses shouted like a football terrace, and lyrics about work, neighborhood and friendship.

The mutation

What sets it apart

The adaptation is the chorus: the audience sings along and becomes part of the band. It differs from hardcore by keeping a danceable tempo and terrace melody.
