Hardcore Punk

Emerged 1979Habitat California and Washington, D.C.Clade Rupture
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Hardcore Punk
The environment

Context

With Reagan in power and first-wave punk turning into fashion, American suburban teens went radical: Bad Brains, Black Flag, Dead Kennedys and Minor Threat. It was a culture of vans, self-run labels, basement shows and straight-edge ethics.

The phenotype

Sound

Extremely fast tempos, shouts instead of singing, d-beat, songs under a minute and pits.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Punk asks for attitude; hardcore asks for physical commitment. It is faster and shorter, stripped of pop traits, with an international DIY network working like a self-contained ecosystem.
