Subgenre of Rock and Roll

The environment

Context

A classic case of geographic isolation: American records reached British ports, and postwar teenagers reinterpreted them in cellars like the Cavern. In 1964 the mutation returned to the US with the Beatles on Ed Sullivan and took over the charts.

The phenotype

Sound

Two guitars, bass and drums, vocal harmonies, immediate choruses and a steady eighth-note beat. It is the lean format that came to define the word band.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It invented the self-sufficient band that writes and plays its own repertoire. It differs from rock'n'roll through its priority on songwriting and vocal harmony, a leap that set up every 1960s branch.
