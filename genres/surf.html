Surf Rock

Emerged 1960Habitat Southern CaliforniaClade Trunk
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Surf Rock
The environment

Context

It was the soundtrack of California beach youth in the early 1960s, a time of optimism, cars and boards. Dick Dale tested amplifiers to breaking point with Leo Fender, and bands played dance halls called stomps.

The phenotype

Sound

Guitar soaked in spring reverb, frantic alternate picking, Arabic-flavored scales and galloping drums. Vocals are optional: much of the repertoire is instrumental, and the Beach Boys added the harmonies.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It differs from rock'n'roll through instrumental guitar in the lead and reverb as a voice in itself. It was an environmental mutation: the sound tries to imitate moving water.
