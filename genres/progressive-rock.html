Progressive Rock

Emerged 1968Habitat UKClade Expansion
Folk → Folk Rock → Psychedelic Rock → Progressive Rock
The environment

Context

In late-1960s Britain, musicians with classical training and labels willing to take risks put rock into the concept-album format. The idea was that rock could compete with classical music in ambition.

The phenotype

Sound

Odd time signatures, tempo changes, twenty-minute suites, Mellotron and Moog keyboards, virtuoso players and lyrics of fantasy or philosophy.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It inherited expansion from psychedelia but replaced chance with rigorous composition. It differs from art rock through declared virtuosity, and this complexity made it punk's perfect target.
