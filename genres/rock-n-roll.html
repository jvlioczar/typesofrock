The environment

Context

The explosion came when young Americans with pocket money, portable radios and no desire to hear their parents' music found Black music through DJs like Alan Freed. Amid segregation, the genre mixed audiences and became the first great moral panic of teen culture.

The phenotype

Sound

Snare backbeat, two-note guitar riffs, hammered piano, sax and two-minute songs about cars, school and dancing. The form is still twelve-bar blues, only in a hurry.

The mutation

What sets it apart

This is the speciation event that names the tree. It took R&B and added a teenage audience, the guitar as protagonist and a rebel image. That package, more than any note, is what defines rock.
