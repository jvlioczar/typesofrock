Rhythm and Blues

Emerged 1945Habitat Postwar US citiesClade Roots
Blues → Rhythm and Blues
The environment

Context

The term replaced "race records" on the 1949 charts and named urban Black music of the postwar years. With a booming economy, big bands shrank into small, loud combos perfect for club dancing and jukeboxes.

The phenotype

Sound

A heavy backbeat, screaming saxophone, boogie-woogie piano, electric guitar and strong vocals. The pulse comes from jump blues, a swing inheritance sped up for the dance floor.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is the missing link that was never missing: the passage from blues to rock'n'roll. It differs from blues through its focus on dancing and a full band, and from rock'n'roll mainly in audience and in the industry's segregation.
