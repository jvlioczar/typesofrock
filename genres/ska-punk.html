Ska Punk

Emerged 1985Habitat UK and CaliforniaClade Rupture
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Ska Punk
The environment

Context

In 1979 England the 2 Tone label put Black and white musicians side by side amid racial tension. Its encounter with California hardcore yielded a hybrid that burst onto American radio in the 1990s.

The phenotype

Sound

Off-beat guitar, punchy horns, bouncing bass, alternating with fast punk sections.

The mutation

What sets it apart

A direct example of hybridization from outside the tree. It differs from pop-punk through horns and Jamaican syncopation.
