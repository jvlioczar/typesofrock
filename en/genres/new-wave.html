The environment

Context

The industry needed a sellable term for more accessible post-punk music. With MTV in 1981 and cheaper synthesizers, image and video became a new selective pressure.

The phenotype

Sound

Synthesizers, clean ska-style guitars, tight beats, clever melodies and ironic vocals.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It shares its origin with post-punk but went the opposite way, toward radio. By trading heavy guitar for synth it approaches pop without losing punk's wit.
