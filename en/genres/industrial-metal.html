The environment

Context

In Chicago, Ministry swapped synth-pop for steel. In 1990 Godflesh and Nine Inch Nails hardened that formula, and Germany's Rammstein proved its stadium success with real pyrotechnics.

The phenotype

Sound

Mechanically precise riffs, electronic drums, samples, synth and processed vocals.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It crosses industrial's machine with thrash's weight. It differs from standard metal through rigidity: here the program, not the drummer, commands the rhythm.
