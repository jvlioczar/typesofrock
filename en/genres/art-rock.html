The environment

Context

By the late 1960s rock was being treated as Art with a capital A: Pet Sounds and Sgt. Pepper pointed the way and Andy Warhol sponsored the Velvet Underground. British art schools supplied part of the musicians.

The phenotype

Sound

It has no single sound. It combines orchestra, noise, unusual forms, concepts and literary lyrics, with artistic intent above formula.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It differs from progressive because it does not always seek virtuosity or long tracks: the Velvets played badly on purpose. What defines art rock is the experimental stance, which is why its genes turn up in glam, post-punk and indie.
