New Wave of British Heavy Metal

Emerged 1977Habitat Northern and central EnglandClade Weight
Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Heavy Metal → New Wave of British Heavy Metal
The environment

Context

When punk seemed to have buried metal, young provincial Britons answered with home tapes, independent singles and the Friday Rock Show. The press named the movement in 1979.

The phenotype

Sound

Harmonized twin guitars, the bass gallop, near-punk speed, high vocals and epic themes.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It absorbed punk's speed and DIY without abandoning virtuosity. It was the incubator of modern metal: without this branch there is no Metallica, and from there much of the extreme tree.
