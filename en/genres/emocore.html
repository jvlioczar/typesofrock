The environment

Context

Bands like Rites of Spring brought vulnerability to the rigid hardcore scene. The name began as a jibe. It went through a cult phase in the 1990s and in the 2000s it exploded through MySpace, fringes and My Chemical Romance.

The phenotype

Sound

Delicate passages and explosions, vocals on the verge of tears, melodic guitars and, in the 2000s wave, arena choruses and polished production.

The mutation

What sets it apart

The mutation was emotional: hardcore began talking about the self, not the system. It differs from pop-punk through drama and sadness, and from post-hardcore through its focus on confession.
