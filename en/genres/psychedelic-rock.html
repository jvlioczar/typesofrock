Psychedelic Rock

Emerged 1965Habitat San Francisco and LondonClade Expansion
Folk → Folk Rock → Psychedelic Rock
The environment

Context

It arrived with LSD, the counterculture, Haight-Ashbury hippies and Swinging London. Opposition to Vietnam and the 1967 Summer of Love created an environment in which music wanted to alter consciousness, with the studio treated as an instrument.

The phenotype

Sound

Reversed tapes, sitar and Indian scales, fuzz and wah-wah, long tracks, drones and dreamlike lyrics. Instruments keep changing texture, as if the sound never stopped flowing.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It was the great adaptive radiation on the tree: progressive, art rock, krautrock and part of heavy metal all come out of it. It stands out by aiming to produce a sensory experience rather than only a song.
