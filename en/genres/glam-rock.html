Glam Rock

Emerged 1971Habitat LondonClade Expansion
Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Glam Rock
The environment

Context

In the hangover of hippie ideals and with Britain in crisis, T. Rex and David Bowie answered with fantasy. Glitter, platforms and androgyny became provocation in a country that had only partly decriminalized homosexuality in 1967.

The phenotype

Sound

Crunchy guitars, marching drums, handclaps, terrace-chant choruses and a full production. Musically it is 1950s rock'n'roll run through glitter, with characters instead of singers.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is a case of sexual selection in rock's evolution: display became the main trait, like a peacock's tail. It differs from hard rock through irony and theatricality, which matter as much as weight.
