Blues Rock

Emerged 1964Habitat London and ChicagoClade Trunk
Blues → Blues Rock
The environment

Context

Young British players collected Chicago records with near-religious devotion. Bands like the Yardbirds, Cream and the Bluesbreakers sent amplified blues back to the US, where it had been marginalized, and American artists like Paul Butterfield took the same road.

The phenotype

Sound

Tube-overdriven guitar, long pentatonic solos, wide vibrato, expansive bass and drums, and twelve-bar structures that become launch pads for improvisation.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It differs from blues through its volume and solo length, and from hard rock by still submitting to the form and feel of blues. It is the bottleneck through which virtuoso guitar entered rock.
