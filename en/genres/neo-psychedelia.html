Neo-Psychedelia

Emerged 1979Habitat Liverpool and Los AngelesClade Alternative
Folk → Folk Rock → Psychedelic Rock → Neo-Psychedelia
The environment

Context

After punk, youths in Liverpool and Los Angeles's Paisley Underground rediscovered 1960s records. With Tame Impala, the sound reached 2010s festivals without needing drugs as an argument.

The phenotype

Sound

Heavily processed guitars, echoed vocals, dreamlike melodies and, in recent waves, synths and home production.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is an open atavism, but filtered through the punk generation: songs are shorter and less jam-based than in original psychedelia.
