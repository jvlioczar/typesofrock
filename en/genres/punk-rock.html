Punk Rock

Emerged 1974Habitat New York and LondonClade Rupture
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock
The environment

Context

Ramones at CBGB in New York, Sex Pistols in London: unemployment, crisis and boredom in the face of a rock that had turned millionaire and remote. Do-it-yourself was the answer to the industry, with indie labels, fanzines and gigs anywhere.

The phenotype

Sound

Three chords with pure downstrokes, high speed, two-minute songs, defiant vocals and raw production. Solos are rare and usually irrelevant.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is the perfect example of punctuated equilibrium: sudden change after years of stability. It differs from garage through ideological awareness and works as a direct reaction to progressive. Stripping down was the mutation.
