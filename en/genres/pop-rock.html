Pop Rock

Emerged 1963Habitat UK and USAClade Trunk
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Pop Rock
The environment

Context

It emerged when labels realized guitars could rule the charts as much as crooners. AM radio, television and singles made it rock's most profitable format for six decades.

The phenotype

Sound

Clean or lightly driven guitars, verse-chorus structure, careful production and melody always up front.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Weight is only seasoning: everything serves the hook. It differs from power pop through less attack and from pure pop by keeping band and guitar at the center. It is the species best adapted to the commercial environment.
