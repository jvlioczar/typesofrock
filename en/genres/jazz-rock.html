Jazz Rock / Fusion

Emerged 1967Habitat New York and LondonClade Expansion
Folk → Folk Rock → Psychedelic Rock → Jazz Rock / Fusion
The environment

Context

Young audiences were moving to rock, and jazz players, led by Miles Davis on Bitches Brew, plugged in. At the same time, rock bands were hiring horn sections.

The phenotype

Sound

Long improvisation, jazz meters and harmony over an electric groove, a prominent bass, Fender Rhodes and fast guitars.

The mutation

What sets it apart

The gene exchange went both ways. It parts from progressive rock through improvisation in place of fixed composition.
