Doom Metal

Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Heavy Metal → Doom Metal
The environment

Context

As metal sped up, some bands clung to the opening track of Black Sabbath's first record. Saint Vitus and Candlemass set the course, and in the early 1990s England produced its most funereal strain.

The phenotype

Sound

Very slow tempo, very low tuning, repeated riffs that feel like physical weight, despairing vocals and lyrics about endings and religion.

The mutation

What sets it apart

Metal's whole arms race was toward speed. Doom chose the opposite selective pressure. It runs the opposite way from thrash while sharing the same ancestor.
