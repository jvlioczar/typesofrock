Grindcore

Emerged 1985Habitat EnglandClade Extreme
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Hardcore Punk → Grindcore
The environment

Context

In Birmingham, the same city as metal, Napalm Death radicalized political hardcore. The 1987 album Scum won fame with help from the BBC's John Peel and has one of the shortest tracks ever released, "You Suffer", at about a second.

The phenotype

Sound

Nonstop blast beats, hoarse and guttural vocals, indistinguishable guitars and songs lasting seconds.

The mutation

What sets it apart

The logical end of shortening: there is no melody and form almost vanishes. It differs from death metal through its punk origin, its politics and its total refusal of technique as display.
