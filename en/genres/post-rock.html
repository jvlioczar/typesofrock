The environment

Context

The term, used in The Wire in 1994, described bands using rock instruments without intending to sound like rock. At the same time, Talk Talk's departure from pop pointed the way.

The phenotype

Sound

Slow builds, no chorus and often no vocals, with a crescendo whose climax works like an explosion.

The mutation

What sets it apart

The great trait is loss: voice, verse and chorus disappeared. Like blind fish in caves, each element without a function was abandoned.
