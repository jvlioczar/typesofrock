Stoner Rock

Emerged 1990Habitat Palm Desert, CaliforniaClade Weight
Blues → Blues Rock → Hard Rock → Heavy Metal → Doom Metal → Stoner Rock
The environment

Context

In the California desert, bands like Kyuss played generator parties far from any scene. Those gatherings birthed a slow rock resonating with Black Sabbath, alongside Monster Magnet on the East Coast and Queens of the Stone Age later.

The phenotype

Sound

Low tunings, heavy bass, exaggerated fuzz, hypnotic repeated riffs and melodic vocals.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It inherits weight and slowness from doom but without despair: the mood is warm and psychedelic. An environmental adaptation that recovers the 1970s more than it modernizes them.
