Slowcore

Emerged 1989Habitat USAClade Alternative
Blues → Rhythm and Blues → Rock and Roll → Beat / British Invasion → Garage Rock → Punk Rock → Post-Punk → Indie Rock → Slowcore
The environment

Context

At grunge's peak, while everyone turned up, Galaxie 500, Codeine and Low turned down almost to silence. It was a refuge of melancholy for a generation of students.

The phenotype

Sound

Slow tempos, much space, few notes, whispered drums and near-spoken voices.

The mutation

What sets it apart

It is extreme reduction with the opposite sign to doom: slow, without weight. Silence works as an instrument, making it a direct forerunner of post-rock.
